How billing works

Every subscription is charged on the 20th of the month. Subscribe by the 20th and that month's charge covers the kit mailed for the 1st of the next month. Subscriptions started on the 21st or later begin the month after. Cancel anytime before the 20th.

Cancellations & refunds

You can cancel anytime from the link above. Cancel before the 20th and you won't be charged for the next month's kit. Because kits are prepared and mailed right after the 20th, charges for kits already prepared or shipped can't be refunded. If your kit arrives damaged or never arrives, email me and I'll make it right. Gift a Year and custom banners are made just for you and are non-refundable once your order is in progress.
